I drew this site in Procreate, and had Claude build it. 

Given that I have no patience for CSS, the code Claude generated resulted in a site that looks better than what I would have done.  

If I had to do it all myself, I probably wouldn't have ended up with a site that looks like this. Instead it would have been much simpler, more utilitarian, less cute, and, I hate to say, _less me._

But I definitely feel like I missed the chance to do some learning. 

As an artist and a software developer, I can envision and implement the site that I want. However, my creativity is limited by how much time I'm willing to dedicate to the tedious job of crafting HTML and CSS. 

In the past, I probably would start with a very rough mockup, then I would have had to look into HTML best practices, cobbled together something very simple, something functional. 

Then I would have had to look into the CSS - I probably would have tried to find a site that was close to what I wanted, inspected it, aped the CSS, and tweaked it to suit. 

_I'm sure it would be a compromise between what I wanted and what I had the patience to do._

Then I would think about adding some behaviour with JavaScript, but at that point I would have spent hours if not days doing research and tweaking, so I'd probably just give up on adding any for now.

Now, I just drew the site I wanted and got back a result in minutes. Less than an hour to get up the home page, a few more hours to design and implement the blog view (I probably spent equivalent time on designing the site and actually implementing it), and the results... I'm impressed.

_And scared._

Because the brain is a muscle, use it or lose it.

We went from working on the ground to sitting at desks. We sacrifice our precious free time to go to the gym to train our physical muscles. 

But what about the brain? If your job - your 9-to-5 job - previously involved using those cognitive muscles, now that you can throw everything at AI... ...what will happen to the muscle that is your brain? 

...

By the way, all the graphics and writing on this site are manually done by me, no AI generation there! Because while Claude (and AI) can be great, there's value in reading code, there's value in evaluating, there's value in thinking and there's value in _creating_. 

And while I missed out on some of the learnings I could have had, if I had tackled everything manually, because I was able to speed through those parts there were other things I could do and learn.

In the end, it's all a compromise.
